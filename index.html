    Scholarships for the trip

    Nobody loves a scholarship form. We don't either. But what we love is that GCC just awarded the NZ/Australia January 2027 Study Abroad trip several partial student scholarships! 😍 Plus, three study abroad scholarships that we feel pretty sure most of you qualify for are out right now and due soon! Get your $$$!

    Super important deadline stuff: To get one of the GCC partial scholarships, you need to have applied, interviewed, and paid your $500 deposit by September 15.     Four scholarships to apply to right now

    GCC's fund (ours). Fill out this Google form; it should take you about 10-15 minutes. Most awards are between $300 and $2,500. We read these in the order they come in, first come first served, and we will get back to you by 9/15. So do this one now (while this page is open 😂).

    Fund for Education Abroad. Typically a few thousand dollars. If you're a citizen, permanent resident, or DACA recipient, this is your first time studying abroad, and you receive financial aid, you qualify for this! Closes Wednesday, September 16 at 9am our time (so really Tuesday night), so it's the one to do right after ours. Apply here

    Gilman Scholarship. Up to $5,000 if you get a Pell Grant. It's federal, it's big, and a lot of people who qualify never apply because they assume it's for some other student. But it's for you! Many GCC students have received one. Deadline October 1. Apply here

    Community College Access Scholarship. Up to $5,000, community college students only, and it covers your passport, visa, and gear, not just the program fee. Deadline October 1. Apply here

    The awards are stackable, so if money is a stress point with the trip, apply to all you qualify for in the hopes of getting more covered.

    Aaaand, the awkward part 🐧: Except for the GCC scholarship, you won't have an answer on the other scholarship applications until after our program deadline. So the $500 deposit does need to go in by September 15 either way, based on what you know today (plus our answer by September 15, if you apply to the GCC scholarship by September 12).

    If you'd like help applying, reach out to the study abroad office (Tirsa and Emily: eshartrand@glendale.edu, tchavez@glendale.edu), but also reach out to us! We can walk you through applying, answer questions about the program, or just cheer you on!

    We leave in January (aka summer in NZ/Aus 🌞). We could not be more excited about taking this journey with you!
